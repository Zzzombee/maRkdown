# Incremental evidence updates

Read the existing AGENTS.md and follow its minimum-context policy. Locate relevant IDs, experimental conditions and direct links before reading passages. Do not read all three state files merely because they exist. Preserve IDs, block IDs, human edits, negative results and uncertainty.

If importing PPTX evidence, first complete [pptx-evidence.md](pptx-evidence.md); otherwise reuse already verified task evidence. Do not run slide extraction for an SOL-only or prose-only task.

## Apply only affected updates

- Record direct measurements and observations in CURRENT_STATE.md. Keep interpretation out of OBS; CON links its OBS and states evidence strength, support limits and relevant alternatives; HYP links the observations/questions it explains and remains unverified.
- Consolidate repeated experimental settings in system/analysis sections, while keeping observations separate when they support different conclusions.
- Update RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md only when evidence changes a question or its priority. Derive questions from missing controls, contradictions, competing mechanisms or consequential decisions, not merely a slide's future-work list. Prioritize discriminating power and decision value.
- Maintain an existing SOL only when its feasibility, target decision, evidence or status is affected; retain its protected core_route. New SOL files require a user request for a specified RQ and [solution-workflow.md](solution-workflow.md).
- Update PROJECT_STORY.md only when the evidence affects its viability, ranking or gaps; then read [story-workflow.md](story-workflow.md).
- Update date for materially changed research notes. Keep extraction provenance in the work directory unless the user requests it in durable notes.
